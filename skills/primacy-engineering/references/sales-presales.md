# sales-presales

Draft discovery, fit-gap, synthetic demo and proposal scope.

## Trigger and boundary

Use for requests such as “Prepare a manufacturing discovery demo”. Select another route for unrelated work. This route supplies procedures and grants no runtime permissions.

## Required context

Authorized notes, approved product facts, current pricing source. Identify the exact version, edition and installed module set when Odoo is involved. Read only authorized files and use installed, approved tools. If a required runtime or service is unavailable, complete safe local preparation and record NOT_RUN or BLOCKED.

## Workflow

Extract industry, users, pain and outcomes. Mark standard, configured, existing-custom, new-custom, excluded and unknown. Draft synthetic demo and scope handoff. Verify pricing and claims with approved sources. Do not invent discounts, timelines or ROI. Obtain approval for outreach and CRM writes.

## Output and validation

Fact-linked draft, assumptions and delivery acceptance. Use [task and evidence templates](../assets/task-evidence.md). Preserve unresolved questions and distinguish static source evidence from live behavior. Reuse existing test recipes after verifying their scope and current revision.
