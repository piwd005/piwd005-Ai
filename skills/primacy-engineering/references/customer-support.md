# customer-support

Triage support and draft verified customer updates.

## Trigger and boundary

Use for requests such as “Triage a staging connector outage”. Select another route for unrelated work. This route supplies procedures and grants no runtime permissions.

## Required context

Impact, entitlement/resource assignment, approved fresh logs. Identify the exact version, edition and installed module set when Odoo is involved. Read only authorized files and use installed, approved tools. If a required runtime or service is unavailable, complete safe local preparation and record NOT_RUN or BLOCKED.

## Workflow

Verify assigned resources and reproduce in an authorized environment. Inspect the reported Policy Engine, Action Registry, Operation Queue and Primacy Agent before use. Keep public advisors separate from operational permissions. RAG cannot authorize. Preserve authorized deterministic actions during model outages. Verify recovery and draft a redacted update.

Return impact and assigned-resource/entitlement checks, evidence-backed cause or clearly
labeled hypotheses, the authorized next action, recovery verification and regression follow-up.
When logs or runtime access are absent, mark impact/cause/recovery unknown and propose checks
rather than stopping at a log request. Do not assume reported queue or agent components exist;
inspect their owning source and scope before using them. Draft customer updates for review.

Use this compact handoff even when evidence is missing. Include every item, marking facts
unknown and actions proposed where appropriate.

- Impact and owner — affected workflow and assigned-resource/entitlement check.
- Cause — confirmed evidence or explicitly labeled hypotheses.
- Next authorized action — scoped diagnostic or recovery proposal.
- Recovery verification — a synthetic connector round trip, backlog reconciliation and no duplicate effects.
- Regression follow-up — reproduce the confirmed failure on redacted fixtures and assert recovery/error handling.
- Customer update — a draft that distinguishes reported outage from verified recovery.

## Output and validation

Impact, cause, approved action, result and regression follow-up. Use [task and evidence templates](../assets/task-evidence.md). Preserve unresolved questions and distinguish static source evidence from live behavior. Reuse existing test recipes after verifying their scope and current revision.
