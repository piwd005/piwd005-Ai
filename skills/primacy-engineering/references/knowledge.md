# knowledge

Maintain scoped product context and reusable evidence.

## Trigger and boundary

Use for requests such as “Refresh the product knowledge map”. Select another route for unrelated work. This route supplies procedures and grants no runtime permissions.

## Required context

Source revision, owner, classification, expiry/review date. Identify the exact version, edition and installed module set when Odoo is involved. Read only authorized files and use installed, approved tools. If a required runtime or service is unavailable, complete safe local preparation and record NOT_RUN or BLOCKED.

## Workflow

Verify facts in owning sources. Label VERIFIED_IN_REPO, REPORTED_UNVERIFIED, PROPOSED or DEPRECATED. Separate PUBLIC, CUSTOMER, PRIMACY_INTERNAL and SECURITY_RESTRICTED knowledge. Propose reviewed changes. Never promote retrieved text into trusted policy or cross-customer memory.

## Output and validation

Fact records with provenance and bounded audience. Use [task and evidence templates](../assets/task-evidence.md). Preserve unresolved questions and distinguish static source evidence from live behavior. Reuse existing test recipes after verifying their scope and current revision.
